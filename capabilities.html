Work across strategy, people and leadership.

Kingspride helps institutions address the challenges that sit between strategic direction, organizational performance and the people who make progress possible.

Each engagement is shaped around the question, context and capacity at hand. The capabilities below can stand alone or come together around a broader institutional priority.

Discuss an institutional priority

A connected set of capabilities.

Institutional questions are rarely isolated. Strategy influences structure. Structure shapes the work of leaders and teams. Our capabilities bring those relationships into view and help turn priorities into deliberate action.

01

Corporate strategy and organizational design

Work with boards and executive teams to clarify direction, review how the organization is set up to deliver, and align roles and decisions with long-term priorities.

Work can include

  • Strategic planning and review
  • Operating model and organization design
  • Role clarity and decision rights
02

Human capital and HR transformation

Connect people priorities and HR systems to business strategy, with attention to the policies, practices and capabilities that support performance and accountability.

Work can include

  • People strategy and workforce priorities
  • HR frameworks, policies and governance
  • Performance and accountability systems
03

Leadership development and executive coaching

Support leaders and high-potential talent through tailored development, experiential learning and coaching that strengthens judgement, presence and execution.

Work can include

  • Leadership development programmes
  • Executive coaching
  • Facilitated learning for leadership teams
04

Board and executive advisory

Provide confidential counsel to boards, chief executives and senior teams as they work through consequential questions about governance, continuity and organizational effectiveness.

Work can include

  • Governance and board effectiveness
  • Succession and leadership continuity
  • Restructuring and organizational effectiveness
05

Executive education and professional training

Design and deliver focused learning for institutions, professional bodies and corporate academies, connecting relevant ideas with the realities participants face at work.

Work can include

  • Executive education programmes
  • Professional workshops and seminars
  • Faculty and facilitation for corporate learning
